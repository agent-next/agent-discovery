# Benchmark task statement

> characterize the RT and predict its function: report every feature you judge
> important, the evidence for it, how confident you are, and what experiment
> would test your main prediction.

## Context (faithful paraphrase, not verbatim)

- The loci were drawn from a large sequence collection because each encodes a
  member of one related group of RTs; nothing else is known about them.
- Each attempt ends with a report and a structured submission of findings.
- Each finding has the form `{claim, evidence, confidence}` with confidence in
  [0, 1].
